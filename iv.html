THE PRINCIPLE

“Space is not empty, it is waiting.”

Our intent is not to fill your life with noise, but to highlight the spaces in between. IV objects are stripped of ornamentation, revealing the raw beauty of hand-poured concrete, cold brass, wild cotton, and unrefined botanical oils.

I. SUBTRACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

We strip away formulation chemicals, noisy packaging details, and fleeting trends. What remains is pristine intentionality.

II. ATMOSPHERIC COHERENCE

Every fragrance, apparel piece, and vessel we create integrates into a single system of atmospheric luxury designed for deep relaxation.

THE NARRATIVE

Born in the margins, built for eternity.

IV was founded as a design counter-response to contemporary noise. We found ourselves constantly searching for products that did not scream for attention, but rather offered a quiet harbor of sensory relief.

Every material we source is selected with heavy ethical parameters and physical resonance. Our linen is grown in sustainable fields across Belgium; our concrete is mixed, cast, and sealed by hand in our studio; and our botanical raw ingredients are sourced directly from certified organic family farms.

“There is a specific joy in deliberate restraint. In knowing exactly when to stop designing.”

— IV Design Director
