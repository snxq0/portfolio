THE VERTEX METHOD

Designing the Space
Between Light & Timber

At ATELIER VÉRTEX, our focus is absolute clarity. We avoid temporary design trends, preferring architectural expressions that transcend generations. Every project represents an integrated response to the geographical profile of its site, capturing native light patterns and utilizing materials that age with elegance.

We utilize high-performance exposed architectural concrete, cedar slatting, raw granite, and untreated travertine. This material honesty crafts structures that feel unified with their earthbound foundations.
Daylight is our primary construction material. Through careful solar mapping, we strategically carve out lightwells, voids, and custom floor-to-ceiling dynamic glazed systems that transform throughout the day.
True luxury is silent. We design advanced thermodynamic acoustic isolation barriers and layout zones that prevent exterior noise while organizing distinct separation between dynamic guest zones and private family chambers.
